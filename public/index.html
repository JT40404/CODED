What it is

CODED is a fee router for tokens launched on pump.fun. You write the rules once; it runs them every claim cycle.

S.02

Modules

Pick the routes that fit your token. Mix them in any ratio that adds up to 100.

Holder split

Pay a share of every claim to wallets holding your token, pro rata to their balance.

Auto-liquidity

Pair fees with your token and add them to the pool, then lock or burn the LP tokens.

Buyback and burn

Buy your token from the pool with fees and send it to the burn address. Supply only goes down.

Creator share

Keep a fixed slice for yourself, a team multisig or a project treasury.

Custom route

Send a percentage to any address you name: marketing, a charity, a partner.

Try ratios with live numbers before you commit anything on-chain.

S.04

Security

CODED never takes custody. The program can only move fees along the routes your config names.

The split between CODED, the creator and any custom wallets is written into pump.fun at launch and can never change. Inside CODED's share, changing the ratios starts a public 48-hour countdown, so holders always see what is coming.

P.04Security

    

      
Rules you canread and check.

      
A fee router is only useful if holders believe it. These are the guarantees the program enforces.

    

  

  

    
No custody
Between claims, fees sit in a vault controlled only by the CODED program. It has no private key, so nobody can sign funds out of it. Only the program's own rules can move them.

    
Public config
Every route, ratio and threshold is stored on-chain and shown on a public page for your token. Anyone can compare what was promised with what each claim did.

    
Split locked at launch
The share going to CODED, the creator and custom wallets is set once in pump.fun's fee sharing, and the right to change it is revoked in the same step. Nobody can change it afterwards.

    
48-hour timelock
Inside CODED's share, the creator can rebalance holders, liquidity and burn. Every change waits 48 hours in public before it applies, and the creator can renounce this right to make the ratios permanent.

    
Slippage caps
Buys are sized to the pool's depth and refused if the price has moved too far from a slow-moving reference price. That cycle's share waits for the next claim instead of being spent into a thin or manipulated pool.

    
Checkable payouts
Every holder payout list is published with its merkle root. Anyone can recompute it from the snapshot, and a bad list can be vetoed during a one-hour window before claims open.

    
Audited before launch
The program will be independently audited before mainnet, and the reports will be published here in full.

  

  

    
What CODED cannot do

    
    
      
  • Mint new tokens or change your token's supply, other than burning what it buys back.
    • 
      
  • Touch tokens or SOL in any wallet, including yours. It only handles claimed creator fees.
    • 
      
  • Change pump.fun's trading fees, bonding curve or pool settings. It decides where your share goes, not how much it is.
    • 
      
  • Send fees anywhere that isn't listed in your published config.
    • 
      
  • Override pump.fun itself. pump.fun's own admins keep powers over coins on their platform, such as community takeovers.

D.02
FAQ

    
Common questions.

  

  

    
Does CODED work before my token graduates?
Yes. Holder split, buyback and burn, creator share and custom routes all work on the bonding curve. Auto-liquidity needs the PumpSwap pool, so its share builds up until the coin graduates and is added from then on.

    
Who pays the network fees for claims?
Network fees for each claim are taken from the claimed amount before it is split. Longer claim intervals mean fewer transactions and less spent on fees.

    
How are holder payouts calculated?
At each claim, CODED takes a snapshot of balances above your minimum threshold, removes excluded accounts, and pays each wallet its share of the holder route in proportion to its balance.

    
Can I change the split later?
Partly. The share going to CODED, you and any custom wallets is permanent from launch. Inside CODED's share you can rebalance holders, liquidity and burn, and every change waits 48 hours in public first.

    
Is CODED part of pump.fun?
No. CODED is an independent tool that works with the creator fees pump.fun pays out. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by pump.fun.

    
Does CODED charge a fee?
Yes: 1% of the creator fees of every coin launched through CODED is used to buy back and burn the CODED token. It's written into each coin's pump.fun fee split at launch, so it's public and permanent, and the CODED program checks it on-chain before routing any of that coin's fees.
